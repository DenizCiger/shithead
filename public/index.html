SETTINGS

HOW TO PLAY

Get rid of all your cards. The last player holding cards is the shithead. Play your hand first, then your face-up cards, then your face-down cards blind.

CARDWHAT IT DOES
3 5 6 9 Q K APlay equal or higher than the top card.
2Always playable. Resets the pile: anything goes next.
3Always playable. Transparent: the card under it counts.
4Reverses direction.
7Next player must play 7 or lower. 6s may be followed by 7s.
8Skips the next player (stacks).
10Always playable. Burns the pile; you play again.
JNext player must play a J (or a 2, 3, 10, Joker).
JOKERHand the whole pile to anyone, even a player who is out. If they hold a Joker they can parry it back at you.

Same-rank cards can be played together. Four in a row burns the pile. Can't play? Pick up the pile.
